# 18.06 Linear Algebra (MIT, Spring 2010, Prof. Gilbert Strang)
Source: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/ (MIT OpenCourseWare, CC BY-NC-SA)

Text: Strang, Introduction to Linear Algebra. Prerequisite: multivariable calculus.
Goals: solving Ax = b by elimination, the four fundamental subspaces, least squares, Gram-Schmidt and QR, determinants, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, symmetric and positive definite matrices, the SVD, and applications.
